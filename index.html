01 · The brand

Scent with a soundtrack

A perfume is a mood.

The personality

Each scent is a type of person, not a list of notes. People pick the one that sounds like them.

The playlist

Every scent has its own playlist on Apple Music and Spotify, behind a QR code on the card inside each order.

The size

15ml sprays: easy to carry, easy to gift, and a low price to try a new scent.

Brand lines

Why it can work

On Takealot, small perfumes are mostly unbranded gift sets or "inspired by" copies. Amora has a story they don't: a personality to pick and a playlist to play. That's easy to remember and easy to post.

It also connects to the body polish: one Amora world, from scrub to scent.

03 · Packaging

The card is the clever part

The bottle is simple. The playlist card is what people remember and post.

Bottle: 15ml spray, same bottle for every scent. Only the label colour changes, so it stays cheap and still looks like a set.

Playlist card: tucked inside every order, with the QR code for that scent's playlist.

Spray it on→scan the code→press play

For Takealot: each bottle in a small box (so it can't leak or break on the way), and the trio in one gift box.

Small details to discover
  • The playlist card with the QR code
  • A line about the personality on the back of the card
  • On the trio box: "Which one are you?"
  • A "post your vibe" note with an Amora hashtag
Same rule as the scrub

Same bottle, same box size, same card size. Only the colour and words change per scent. That keeps costs low and makes the three look collectible together.

07 · Takealot

Takealot is the main shop

Perfume is a popular gift in November and December. Your perfume already exists, so it can be on Takealot before the body polish.

What you need to sign up
  • Your ID and a bank account. No company needed. One seller account covers the perfume and the polish.
  • A barcode (EAN) for each scent and one for the trio.
  • Product details: name, 15ml, ingredients, scent description.
Perfume rules to check
  • Never use a designer brand name in your title, photos or description, like "inspired by" a famous perfume. Takealot can remove the listing.
  • Alcohol perfume is flammable. Ask Takealot what's needed to send perfume to their warehouse.
  • Bottles must survive the trip. Box each one and test by posting a bottle to a friend.
Photos that win the click
  • Main photo: bottle on a white background, filling most of the frame, at least 1000 × 1000 pixels.
  • Then: the playlist card, the QR being scanned, the trio box, the bottle in a hand for size.
  • Use the personality in the title, for example "Amora The Overthinker Eau de Parfum 15ml".
Getting the first sales
  • Ask past customers to buy their next bottle on Takealot and leave an honest review.
  • Put the Takealot link in your bio and every Reel.
  • Card in the box: "Loved it? Tell others in a Takealot review". Never offer anything in return.
  • Watch the Promotions Calendar for the Blue Dot Sale (Black Friday is 27 November 2026).
  • Try a small Sponsored Ads budget once you have 5 good reviews.

13 · Risks and plan B

What could go wrong, and what you'd do

If this happensPlan B
Takealot removes a listing over a brand nameOnly ever use Amora names and your own scent descriptions. Never mention designer perfumes.
A bottle leaks or breaks in deliveryBox every bottle, tighten the sprayer, and test by posting one to a friend before you send stock.
Customers say it doesn't last longLet it rest longer before selling, or try a stronger oil percentage in a test batch.
One scent sells and the others don'tLead with the best seller on Takealot and push the trio so people discover the others.
Sales are slow in the first monthChange one thing at a time: main photo, price, then title. Check your weekly numbers to see what moved.
Someone has a skin reactionRefund kindly, ask what happened, check the oil's safety sheet. Keep batch records so you can trace it.
Stock runs out before ChristmasMixed perfume needs 2 to 4 weeks to rest, so make December stock by early November.
Bad review on TakealotReply politely and fix the cause. A few honest reviews of any kind are better than none.
