01 · The idea

Let people watch the scent being made

By launch day, they already feel it's partly theirs.

One scent

Not a range. One original perfume you make yourself, so all the attention and all the content goes to it.

Oil-based

No alcohol. A perfume oil in a 50ml bottle: sits close to the skin, lasts and feels personal.

Made in public

Every step becomes content: learning, mixing, failing, testing, naming. That's what makes people care before it exists.

What stays from Amora
  • Perfume with a personality.
  • The playlist card: your original scent gets its own playlist.
  • Handmade in Johannesburg.
  • Don't compare. Just glow.
Why content first

Oil perfumes on Takealot are cheap: Al-Rehab 6ml roll-ons sell for R67 to R89. You can't win on price. You win because people watched you make it and want the one you made.

You're still learning to formulate, and that's the story. Learning in public is honest and easy to film.

02 · The scent journey

From first drop to launch day

Each stage is a chapter of content. Number every trial (Mod 1, Mod 2, Mod 3), the way perfumers do. The numbers become a series people follow.

1 · Learn

Weeks 1 to 3

Top, heart and base notes, what oil-based means, smelling materials on blotter strips. Share what you learn as you learn it.

2 · The idea

Week 3

The mood, the person it's for, the feeling. A mood board and a playlist to set the direction. Let followers vote on the direction.

3 · Mods

Weeks 4 to 9

Small trial batches, each numbered and written down to the drop. Most won't work. The failures are content too.

4 · Testers

Weeks 7 to 10

Friends and followers wear the best mods for a day and report back. Their reactions are your best videos.

5 · Final, name and rest

Weeks 10 to 13

Pick the winner, reveal the name (followers can help choose), make the first batch and let it rest.

6 · Launch

Weeks 14 to 16

Waitlist first, then everyone. Timed for Valentine's 2027 gifting.

03 · Content plan

The content is the plan

Ideas and directions, not scripts. Say it your way.

Reel 1 of 3 · Lesson

What I learned

One thing from this week of making: a note you hate, a mistake, a perfume word explained simply.

Reel 2 of 3 · Interview

Real people smell it

Friends, testers and strangers smell a mod and react honestly. Ask 2 or 3 real questions.

Reel 3 of 3 · Trend

Something different

A trending sound or format applied to perfume-making. Keeps the account fresh and reaching new people.

Series to repeat
  • Mod diaries: "Mod 7: what changed and how it smells now"
  • You decide: polls on direction, bottle, label colour and name
  • Perfume words: one term explained a week (notes, dry down, longevity)
  • Smell test: people react to the latest mod
  • Behind the bottle: real costs, mistakes and wins of building Amora
  • The playlist: followers suggest songs for the scent's playlist
Weekly rhythm
  • 5 feed posts a week, alternating Reels and carousels
  • Reels rotate: lesson → interview → trend
  • One batch day a week to film and edit everything
  • Stories every day: quick polls, a sniff, a drop going in
  • Every post ends with a reason to join the waitlist

What to watch

Before launch, sales don't matter yet. Watch these instead: waitlist sign-ups, DMs saying "I want it", saves and shares, and new followers. If a series gets saves and DMs, make more of it.

04 · Waitlist

Build the list before the bottle

The waitlist is proof people want it and your first-day sales.

1 · Where

A WhatsApp broadcast list or an Instagram "close friends" list. Keep it simple.

2 · Why join

First to buy, first to smell the final mod, a vote on the name, maybe a free sample vial.

3 · Testers

Pick 5 to 10 waitlist people to test mods. They'll talk about it, post it and buy it.

Your "go" rule

When 50 people are on the waitlist, make the first batch. Until then, keep posting and keep improving the scent.

05 · Packaging

A 50ml bottle and a playlist

50ml is a full-size perfume, so the bottle should feel like one. Glass, with a sprayer that works with thin oils (fractionated coconut oil sprays well), or a dropper.

Bottle: 50ml glass. Test that the sprayer works with your oil before you buy many. Buy a few styles early and let followers vote.

Playlist card: the scent's own playlist behind a QR code, tucked into every order. Your signature, carried over.

Spray it on→scan the code→press play

Box: a small box per bottle for gifts and Takealot.

12 · Business setup

Make it official, step by step

If you've done these for the body polish, they count here too.

Money help: NYDA grant

If you're 18 to 35 and a South African citizen, the National Youth Development Agency gives grants that don't need to be paid back, plus mentoring. You apply online, do a business training course and give a 10-minute pitch.

13 · Risks and plan B

What could go wrong, and what you'd do

If this happensPlan B
The scent isn't right yet by week 10Move the launch. Keep posting the mods: the struggle is the most honest content you have.
Posting 5 times a week is too muchDrop to 3 feed posts and keep daily Stories. One batch day a week, no daily filming.
The waitlist grows slowlyLook at which posts got saves and DMs, and make more of those. Give waitlist people a vote or a sample.
It fades fast on skinTry more base notes or a higher concentrate percentage in the next mod.
A tester gets a skin reactionStop using that mod, check each material's safety sheet and limits, and note it.
The oil goes cloudy or smells offUse fractionated coconut or jojoba (both stay fresh), add vitamin E, and store away from heat and light.
Someone copies the ideaYour story, your face and your community can't be copied. Register the Amora trademark.
Takealot sales are slowNormal for a new brand. Keep your waitlist and Instagram as the main shop and use Takealot as extra.
